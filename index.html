CyberFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

CyberFit is an entertainment app built around a satirical virtual workout experience. We respect your privacy and have designed CyberFit to minimize the collection of user data.

Information We Collect

CyberFit does not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell, or share personal information about its users. No account or user registration is required to use the app.

Workout and Waste Record Data

Workout information, completed-session records, and Waste Record progression used by CyberFit are stored locally on the user's device. CyberFit does not transmit this information to our servers.

Deleting the app or its local data may remove locally stored CyberFit records.

In-App Purchases

CyberFit offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. CyberFit does not receive or store your payment card or other financial account information. Apple may process information associated with purchases according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Sharing

CyberFit allows users to share generated workout reports using sharing features provided by the user's device. CyberFit does not operate a social network and does not upload shared reports to our own servers. Information shared through third-party apps or services is subject to the privacy practices of those services.

Advertising and Tracking

CyberFit does not display third-party advertising and does not use advertising tracking technologies.

Health Information

CyberFit is an entertainment app and is not a medical device or healthcare service. Workout, calorie, fitness, and related values displayed by CyberFit are part of the entertainment experience and should not be treated as medical or health data.

Children's Privacy

CyberFit does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if CyberFit's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
